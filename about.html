About Lifeloop 🌐

Lifeloop bridges the gap between people seeking assistance and those willing to provide it.

The Problem

Community requests are often scattered across chaotic WhatsApp groups and social media feeds, where critical messages get buried quickly and valuable opportunities are missed.

Our Solution

Lifeloop centralizes requests into six clear categories (Education, Social Help, Food, Jobs & Skills, Environment, and Community), offering AI-assisted classification and flexible Local or Nationwide reach options.
